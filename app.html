Align Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out when repeating schedules land on the same day from the repeats you type. It does not know about holidays, skipped days, real calendars or how any task should be run, and it gives no verdict on whether a schedule should change. Every number is your own entry.

Your repeats

Each repeat is a period in days, a colon, and the first day it lands (day 1 is the first day). Give 2 to 4, separated by commas. They are called A, B, C and D in order.

The cycle truth

How long until the whole pattern restarts, from the periods alone.

The meeting truth

The first day all of them land together, if there ever is one.

The count truth

How often each one lands, and how often all of them land together, in the days you count.

Align Math is exact arithmetic over the repeats you enter. Why it exists.
