Align Math

A chore every 4 days and another every 6 days, starting on days 1 and 2, never land on the same day.

Boundary: arithmetic only Align Math works out when repeating schedules land on the same day from the repeats you type. It does not know about holidays, skipped days, real calendars or how any task should be run, and it gives no verdict on whether a schedule should change. Every number is your own entry.

Repeats 4:1 and 6:2 (period in days, then first day), counted over 60 days.

The cycle truth

Repeating every 4 and 6 days: the pattern restarts after 12 days, the least common multiple. The greatest common divisor is 2.

The meeting truth

These never land on the same day. Each pair of schedules needs the gap between its first days to be a multiple of the greatest common divisor of its two periods, and at least one pair here is not.

The count truth

In days 1 to 60: A lands 15 times, B lands 10 times. All of them land on the same day 0 times.
